His story

The other road in Calcutta

Hemendranath Mazumdar came to Calcutta from East Bengal and became one of the leading academic painters of his day, working with the light and modelling of European realism.

He is best known for his figures of Bengali women, glowing and often just come from bathing, painted for princely patrons and collectors across India. The Ascetic, in the house, shows his quieter, devotional side.

Where he stands
