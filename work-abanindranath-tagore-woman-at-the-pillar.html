The story of this work

Damayanti pauses at a pillar as a bird alights beside her.

Abanindranath Tagore, the founding figure of the Bengal School, lets the reds and gold of the sari carry the painting against a ground that fades almost to nothing.

Every work in the house can be seen in person, in a quiet room set aside for you.
