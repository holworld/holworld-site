The story of this work

A face drawn almost entirely in tone, with barely a hard line anywhere.

Dhurandhar, shaped by the academic tradition of Bombay's J. J. School of Art, at his most quiet and intimate.

Every work in the house can be seen in person, in a quiet room set aside for you.
