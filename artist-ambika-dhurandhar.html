Her story

A painter in her own right

Ambika grew up in a house full of painting. Her father, M. V. Dhurandhar, took her to the J. J. School himself, and in 1931 she graduated near the top of her class, among the first women to do so.

She painted landscape and architecture with an exact, confident hand, like the pavilion in the house, and kept painting for most of a very long life.

Where she stands

Father and daughter

She learned from her father and painted alongside the J. J. School's next generation.
