His story

From Calcutta to Delhi

Sarada Ukil trained under Abanindranath Tagore and painted in the soft wash of the Bengal School: Buddhist legends, scenes from the epics, and quiet studies of birds and animals.

He settled in Delhi with his brothers Barada and Ranada, both artists too, and in 1926 opened his own art school in the city. It taught generations of Delhi painters and still carries his name.

For a house in Delhi devoted to the Bengal School, he is something of an ancestor.
