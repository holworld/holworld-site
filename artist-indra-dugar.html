His story

Taught at home, and under the trees

Indra Dugar was born into a Jain family in Jiaganj, on the river in Murshidabad. His father, Hirachand Dugar, was one of the first students at Kala Bhavana, and the boy spent much of his childhood at Santiniketan.

He never went to art school. His father taught him, and in the holidays Nandalal Bose, whom everyone called Mastermoshai, gave him private lessons and looked over his work.

Without academic training he found a voice of his own: quiet landscapes of riverside villages and Himalayan evenings, often in tempera, with a soft, matte glow.

Where he stands

Mastermoshai's private pupil

Taught by Nandalal outside the classroom, he went on to work among the painters of Calcutta's Academy of Fine Arts.
