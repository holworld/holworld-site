The story of this work

A woman leans into her task, a rope pulled taut across the sheet, in sharp strokes of blue and red.

The diagonal energy is pure Ramkinkar Baij, who loved to show the body at work.

Every work in the house can be seen in person, in a quiet room set aside for you.
