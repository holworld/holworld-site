The story of this work

A disc, a bar and a single arc balanced in space, ruled with absolute precision.

Nasreen Mohamedi pared drawing back to line and interval, and her work now stands among the most admired of Indian modernism.

Every work in the house can be seen in person, in a quiet room set aside for you.
