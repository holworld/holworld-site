His story

A village, painted for fifty years

Vaikuntam grew up in a village in Karimnagar, where his father kept a grocery shop. As a boy he watched travelling theatre troupes in which men played the women's parts with painted faces, heavy jewellery and bright saris. Those women never left him.

At Baroda, K. G. Subramanyan pushed him to stop looking at European painting and find what was his own. What he found was home: the women and men of Telangana, drawn in a strong charcoal line and filled with pure reds, yellows and greens he refuses to mix.

He has carried the same figures into bronze, painting the metal so the face keeps its colour.
