The story of this work

A white bird pauses against a tree trunk, its feathers built from soft rubbed pastel over ochre.

Gopal Ghose was famous for the speed of his hand, but this is a still and patient sheet.

Every work in the house can be seen in person, in a quiet room set aside for you.
